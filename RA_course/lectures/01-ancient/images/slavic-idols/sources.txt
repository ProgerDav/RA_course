Source/Author Description: Световид / Zbruch Idol (Збручский идол) — 9th–10th century Slavic limestone pagan pillar idol, 257 cm tall, found in the Zbruch river (Galicia) in 1848. Now at the Archaeological Museum in Kraków (accession MAK/63). Photographed and digitized by RDW MIC, Małopolska's Virtual Museums project. The idol features four-sided bas-reliefs depicting Slavic deities across three tiers (underworld, earth, heavens) and is one of the most important surviving monuments of pre-Christian Slavic belief.
